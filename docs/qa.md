## Blockers

**D1. Fog eats the machine in the tower world, at every width.** `tower`, 1280, 768, 390, both languages. The home view stands at the camera's limit, 52 m (measured at all three widths), and `fog: [48, 59]` starts at 48. So the machine, from 49 to 55 m away, is 10 to 60 % fog on its very first screen: the pad is nearly white and the weight, the reason for this world, is a pale ghost (`R/depth/w1280-tower-home.jpg`, `R/depth/w768-tower-home.jpg`, `R/depth/w390-tower-home.jpg`; every side: `R/depth/w1280-tower-az120.jpg`). **Tried in the page:** fog from 56 to 59.5 m gives back the carrier, the blocks and the steel at all three widths, with the floor still fading and no clipping seen (`R/depth/w1280-tower-try-fog56.jpg`, `R/depth/w768-tower-try-fog56.jpg`, `R/depth/w390-tower-try-fog56.jpg`). **Fix, modeler, one line:** `tower/scene.ts:56`. **Lead, kit:** request 14 gives real margin. **Fixed in the tree (modeler, on kit 0.6.0):** `far` 140 m, `near` 0.05, camera's limit 72 m, fog from 86 to 135 m, behind the whole machine at that limit, tested; the floor fades by its own radius, so that no hard line crosses a close view (`F/final-060/w1280-tower-main.jpg`, `F/final-060/w768-tower-main.jpg`, `F/final-060/w390-tower-main.jpg`, `F/final-060/w1280-tower-iso-tie.jpg`).
