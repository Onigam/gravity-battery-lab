# The 3D worlds of the Gravity Battery lab

Written by the lead from `src/three`, for the reviewer and the film. Four worlds, 52 parts, every length from `src/three/dims.ts` (metres; each constant says `reported`, `derived` or `estimate`; what the physics needs lives in `src/sim/machine.ts` and is imported, never retyped). `src/three/index.ts` exports the four as `lazyModule`s, so nothing is built until the visitor goes there. The land, plants, buildings and sky of the `site` are in `docs/scenery.md`.

## One machine, one function

`buildMachine` (`rig/machine.ts`) makes the whole machine, in `site`, `tower` and `hoist`, at the same place: the tower stands at the origin of all three. A world only decides which groups it registers as parts. `site` bakes everything that stands still into one mesh per material and winds the rope coarser: the same triangles at the same places outside the winding, and fewer draw calls. `same.test.ts` holds the shared parts within 3 mm across the worlds. The `update` of the machine is the one place that reads the simulation, so the weight, the rope, the sheaves and the brakes do the same thing everywhere.

| File                       | What                                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `rig/structure.ts`         | what stands still: pad, four legs, lattice, rails, buffers, ladder, ruler, head frame with its deck and roof. Every member ends on another one (tested)                            |
| `rig/weight.ts`            | what moves with the weight: carrier and sling, blocks, ties, shoes, safety gear, hook block. The load path is drawn link by link: sheaves, hook, crosshead, uprights, deck         |
| `rig/hoist.ts`             | the hoist on the maker's dimensions (S9 p. 1/55), in force order: real solids, so a section shows the gears, the rotor and the inside of the drum                                  |
| `rig/rope.ts`              | one rope: drum, fall 1, sheave A, fall 2, return sheave, fall 3, sheave B, fall 4, socket. Segments are aimed every frame between markers on their parts, in the exploded view too |
| `rig/rope-flows.ts`        | "Flows" in `tower` and `hoist`: the same three paths, read each frame from the ends of the falls, at 4 v, 2 v and 0                                                                |
| `rig/shapes.ts`, `look.ts` | the solids, from dimensions passed in; the materials per world, a smallest width on screen for the rope, the drum's helical groove                                                 |
| `powerroom/room.ts`        | the shed's shell: one function, closed in `site`, open towards the visitor in `power-room`                                                                                         |

Ids and FR/EN names: `docs/research.md` §4, `HOTSPOTS` in each `scene.ts`, the sheets in `src/data/parts`. A landscape to the kit is a world called `site` (far plane 4000 m). `tower` takes `near` 0.05 and `far` 140 m (kit 0.6.0), with the camera's limit at 72 m and fog from 86 to 135 m, behind the whole machine from that limit (tested); the floor fades by its own radius (`fadeFloor`), so that no hard line crosses a close view. `hoist` and `power-room` keep the default 0.01 and 60 m.

**Selecting a part** (kit 0.6.0). `FRAMING` in `tower/scene.ts` and `hoist/scene.ts`: a `frame` for what is too long to read whole (a stretch of a leg, of the bracing, of a rail, of the ladder, of the ruler; the rope's follows the hook block every frame), and a `viewDir` where the home direction has something in front, each chosen by rays over 54 directions. The guides, the envelope and a part shown alone keep the whole part. `declutter` is on in the four worlds: with every label on, a name with no room folds into its dot.

## What each world reads from the simulation

- **The machine**: `zFraction` (never `z`: a preset may have another stroke), `fallLength`, `blocks` and `halves`, `drumAngle`, `sheaveAngle`, `motorAngle`, `motorBlur`, `brakeReleased`, `safetyGearSet`, `mode`. Gears are drawn turning up to 4 rad/s and 14 teeth a second, then a blur disc takes over. The moves of the brakes are drawn larger than life (a few tenths of a millimetre in truth): they say that it happens, not how far.
- **`site`**: `hour` (sun, moon, stars, windows, the tower's lamp), `load`, `pv`, `gridPower`, `chain.meter`, `flowDirection`, `ghostTonnes`, `ghostBlocks`, `ghostTowers`, and through `params` the `wind`, `gateOpen` and `grid`, which the state does not hold (kit request 18).
- **`power-room`**: `flowDirection` and the powers for the flows; `dcVolts` for the link's lamp, which stays on while it holds a voltage; `chopperPower` for the coils; `soc` for the module's bars; `drive`, `alarms` for the lamps of the safety chain.

**The ghost stack** (`site/scene.ts`) is the comparator the owner chose: real blocks on the meadow, in stacks like the machine's, and lattice towers along the far side of the farm, as many as a night takes, the last one as tall as what is left of a tower. It is a translucent copper, a quantity and not a thing. A label stands over it, with it and only with it, and reads the state (« 128 t\*, 6,4 tours\*, pour 3 kWh\* »); clicking it opens the block yard's sheet.

**What belongs to no part.** The governor's rope and its lower sheave, and the motor's cable, are scenery in every world, so the registry does not hide them and they follow no part. `holdScenery` shows them only when every part is there and in its place: isolate or a guide's step hides them, the exploded view too. In `hoist`, where the rope is no part, it goes with them while a part is hidden, and stays exploded, aimed at its markers. A blur disc shows only while the part that holds it is shown (`machine.blurAllowed`).

`lab-kit/tools/review/flicker.mjs`, 150 frames, 1280 × 720, Metal: the share of pixels that do not change smoothly from frame to frame, in a slow orbit / a slow dolly.

| `site`       | 1.80 / 7.55 %                             | 1.77 / 7.55 % (1.33, 1.21)                               |
| `tower`      | 0.28 / 1.78 %                             | 0.13 / 0.97 % (1.27, 1.44)                               |
| `hoist`      | 2.99 / 7.89 %                             | 1.46 / 3.68 % (1.38, 1.59)                               |
| `power-room` | 1.25 / 9.61 %                             | 0.71 / 4.50 % (1.36, 1.32)                               |

A jump over 2 is a pop: none. The lower figures on the right for the three workshop worlds were not caused by any change to those worlds that the lead can name, so take the two columns as the spread of the measure. The cut of the far plants left the `site` where it was.

After the review's fixes, on kit 0.6.0 (2 Nov), the same tool: `site` 2.59 / 7.82 % (1.32, 1.18), `tower` 0.17 / 0.89 % (1.52, 1.39), `hoist` 1.15 / 3.25 % (1.37, 1.37), `power-room` 0.72 / 4.55 % (1.36, 1.32). The leaf sprays cost the `site`'s orbit most of a point (Mill's site, for scale: 2.64, 9.64); no pop. Alpha to coverage on the sprays made it worse (2.99), so it is off.

## Weak points, known and open

- **Labels.** With every label on, `declutter` leaves no overlap in any world at any width. The main labels of `tower` and `hoist` are clean at 1280, 768 and 390, and all four worlds at 1280. The drum brake's label is minor: at the machine's far end it ran off the view at 768 and 390, and a label has no side. Left: the room's main labels at 768 and 390, and « Maison » at 768 and 390.
- **The safety gear.** From its own direction, 21 of 44 rays reach it: it rides inside the sling and the lattice. X-ray or isolate show it. Look at it in motion, on the scenario « Survitesse ».
- **The ghost's label on a phone.** It says the mass and the towers, not the kWh: the whole does not fit to the right of its dot.
- **The farm and the room on narrow screens.** The kit sets the distance by the width. The farm is held at 320 m, with the barn and the house just in the view. The room stays a strip across a tall view.
- **Close-ups at ×600.** A part on the weight is framed where it is when selected: at a fast clock it has moved away a moment later.

## Tests

`dims.test.ts`: the maker's page, what is imported from the physics, the levels adding up, the rope on the drum. `rig/machine.test.ts`: the rope's path, the sense of rotation, nothing floating, contacts measured by rays. `rig/same.test.ts`: the same machine in three worlds, every scenario and preset of the physics, the budgets. `rig/review.test.ts`: what the independent review found, held: nothing left standing under isolate or exploded, the roof with its posts, the groove, the rope clear of the guide, the fog, the farm on the ground, the rope's flows, the gears in section, the ghost's label. `site/site.test.ts` and `powerroom/powerroom.test.ts`: the world's own rules (see `docs/scenery.md`).
