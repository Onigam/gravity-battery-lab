The kit opens one scenario per lesson: the others are named word for word as the panel names them, so that all ten can be reached from a lesson. Each lesson has four paragraphs of at most 95 words, a formula and three chips.

**Honest points.** Lesson 2 gives the round trip as a model result with its range, and the only near-measurement (about 75 %, one block, 2020) with no primary report. Lesson 3 says the fridge can be served, at 5 %, and that the battery that solves it holds 4.4 towers on its own. Lesson 4 says the courtyard's figures are a plausibility. Lesson 6 says the battery wins at this scale, states the sceptics' arithmetic and not their words, names real projects with their dates and no adjective, and says the status ages: a fact-check before each release is still needed.
